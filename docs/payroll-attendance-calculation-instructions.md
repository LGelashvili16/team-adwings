# Attendance-based payroll calculation instructions

Prepared 2026-11-27 for review before implementation. Start from the [payroll delivery checklist](payroll-implementation-plan.md). Reuse the existing [monthly payroll workflow](payroll.md), [attendance and corrections](attendance-review.md), [job assignments](job-assignments.md), and [GPS geofencing plan](gps-implementation-plan.md).

## Fixed monthly salary

- Expect attendance on each working date in the employee's effective schedule, including inherited company schedules. Compare completed, eligible attendance to those dates. Review approved leave, holidays, schedule exceptions and attendance corrections before flagging missed work. Send missing or disputed attendance to HR or an administrator for review. Any automatic salary reduction requires a separate agreed rule.
- Add an effective-dated **automatic rest-day bonuses** switch to each fixed-salary employee's terms or settings. Default it to off for existing and new employees. Generate bonuses only for eligible work dates on which the switch was on. Preserve the switch history and existing manual adjustments.
- Provide a separate employee-level **rest-day clock-in** switch. Enforce it on the server and give the employee a Georgian explanation when a new punch is blocked. When allowed, reuse the existing day-off confirmation. The switch controls starting new rest-day sessions and must let employees close sessions already open. Proposed initial behavior is to grandfather the existing clock-in behavior until an authorized manager changes the setting. The bonus switch controls payment eligibility independently.
- For each eligible rest date with confirmed work, calculate one bonus using `(agreed monthly salary / defined working days in the earning month) × 1.30`. Add the whole result as a bonus to the monthly base. Two sessions on the same rest date yield one bonus. Proposed divisor is the count of scheduled working dates in the full earning month under the effective individual or inherited schedule. Missing history, a zero divisor, a partial month or changing terms require review until their treatment is agreed. Proposed qualification is an approved closed interval on the rest date, with short visits and overnight attribution to be confirmed.
- Automatic bonuses need a Georgian explanation and traceable source. Show the rest date, monthly rate, divisor, multiplier and amount, with links to the supporting attendance for authorized reviewers. Reuse the existing bonus note in the user interface and retain structured calculation evidence. Example note: `დასვენების დღეს მუშაობის ბონუსი (2026-11-21)`. Preserve the existing optional notes for manual bonuses.

**Example.** A monthly salary of 2,200 GEL and 22 working days gives a daily rate of 100 GEL. One eligible rest day adds 130 GEL, for 2,330 GEL before other adjustments. A scheduled day without eligible evidence creates a review item with no automatic deduction.

## Hourly salary

Use each employee's applicable hourly rate and actual eligible worked time. Evaluate pay in the company-local timezone from scheduling, normally `Asia/Tbilisi`. Split intervals at premium-band, local date, earning-month and rate boundaries as needed. Treat each band as including its start and excluding its end. Use exact elapsed seconds from the underlying timestamps, exclude gaps between sessions, and keep monetary calculations in integer tetri with a defined final rounding point. The attendance work date can stay attached to the original session while payroll apportions time.

| Work segment                                                                         | Calculation                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- |
| 10:00 to 21:00 on a scheduled day, within scheduled hours plus up to two extra hours | Base hourly rate × eligible hours        |
| 21:00 to midnight on a scheduled day                                                 | Base hourly rate × eligible hours × 1.10 |
| Midnight to 10:00 on a scheduled day                                                 | Base hourly rate × eligible hours × 1.30 |
| Work on the employee's rest day                                                      | Base hourly rate × eligible hours × 1.30 |

Proposed handling is to apply the two-hour allowance once per workday across all sessions and use it for outside-schedule work in the 10:00 to 21:00 band. Known evening and night segments still use their stated multipliers. Daytime hours above the allowance enter review with their actual duration visible until the additional rate is agreed. Proposed rest-day treatment is a single 30% premium for all rest-day hours, overriding the time-band premium. Confirm the treatment of holidays, leave and work that crosses into a rest date. Keep approval and payout readiness distinct from the calculation. Flag disputed, overlapping, open or provisional intervals for review. Record who confirmed any manually supplied hours and detect overlap with attendance or GPS-derived hours.

**Examples.** At 20 GEL/hour on a scheduled 10:00 to 19:00 day, eligible attendance from 10:00 to 21:00 earns 220 GEL. If both calendar dates are scheduled working days, 20:00 to 01:00 earns `20 + 3 × 22 + 26 = 112 GEL`, assuming the daytime hour fits within the allowance. Two eligible hours on a rest day earn 52 GEL under the proposed non-stacking rule.

## GPS job attendance and hourly pay

1. Use the tracker attached to the job and the saved individual crew. The tracker supplies shared presence evidence for this job. Use the existing circular zone, its saved center and effective diameter, and its radius for the distance check. Resolve the eligible job and crew server-side, with an active/enabled tracker and a valid zone. Capture the job, tracker, zone revision and crew at entry. Route conflicting candidate jobs for the same tracker to review.
2. Use a server-side worker to evaluate saved, authenticated GPS fixes. Confirm entry and exit with freshness, accuracy, boundary tolerance and dwell rules agreed against real device behavior. Store the confirmed transition, supporting fix IDs, observed time and processing time. Use the first qualifying observation in a confirmed transition as the proposed punch time. A first reliable inside fix can start an observed visit at that fix time. A signal gap, invalid fix, disconnect or configuration change must never fabricate a physical crossing or a checkout. Flag uncertain or stale visits for review.
3. On confirmed entry, automatically clock in each eligible assigned employee. On confirmed exit, automatically clock out the same employees' valid GPS-created sessions. The request covers all eligible assigned employees, with GPS hourly earnings applied only to employees on hourly terms. Retain job and visit provenance on the attendance record. Reuse attendance invariants, including the one-open-session rule and overlap checks. An existing overlapping or manual punch requires a reconciliation item and must never be silently closed or counted twice. Record each crew member's outcome and allow unaffected crew members to finish. Review assignments, device, zone or eligibility changes during an open visit before accepting a disputed interval. The job's execution status remains governed by the existing job workflow.
4. At a completed visit, prepare a draft earning for each eligible hourly employee using `(confirmed time in zone + 20 minutes) × that employee's hourly rate × 1.30`. Keep the 20-minute allowance visible as paid time on the earning. The raw attendance timestamps reflect the observed visit. Proposed handling is one allowance per confirmed visit, attributed to the completion date and its hourly rate, with the actual interval split across earning months or rate changes when necessary. Apply the GPS factor once to the job time. These same worked seconds are used only once in payroll. Proposed precedence is to use the GPS formula for qualifying job time, even during night or rest-day hours, and apply standard hourly rules only to other eligible time.
5. Make transitions, attendance commands and earnings retry-safe with stable visit and employee identifiers. Serialize conflicting writes, retain the original GPS evidence and record corrections as revisions. Duplicates, delayed or out-of-order fixes, replay, reconnects and re-entry must never duplicate a clock action, a paid interval or an allowance. Keep receiver acknowledgments tied to durable telemetry storage and process payroll outside the receiver's acknowledgment path.

**Example.** For a confirmed two-hour visit and a rate of 20 GEL/hour, the observed attendance is two hours, payable time is two hours and 20 minutes, and draft earnings are 60.67 GEL. Another eligible crew member can have a different amount from the same visit because their hourly rate differs.

## Review, corrections and payment

- Show the calculation in the existing monthly payroll workflow and link to source attendance or job visits where permitted. Display the date, source, actual and payable time, rate, multiplier, allowance or bonus divisor, amount, review state and reasons. Use the [UI standards](ui-design-guidelines.md) for the existing Georgian interface and correction forms.
- Give HR managers and administrators feature-specific rights to review and correct these drafts and GPS-generated punches within their authorized employee scope. Allow them to add missing evidence, resolve conflicts, adjust hours, override or void a calculated amount, and record a required reason. Keep raw fixes, original punches, before/after values, reviewer, timestamp and source revision. Recalculate from successful time corrections and flag overrides for re-review on relevant source changes. Guard every read and mutation server-side and prevent self-review. HR currently lacks payroll-wide permissions and administrators lack default company-wide attendance correction, so explicitly add the limited grants this workflow needs. Keep current publication permissions in the existing workflow.
- Reuse effective salary terms, confirmed employment dates, and retained schedule versions. Generate or refresh provisional results from committed attendance, review and relevant configuration changes through a retryable server process. Avoid double counting existing confirmed hourly entries and manual adjustments. Preserve exact facts and calculation inputs with a version so that a reviewer can reproduce any result. Approving a draft leaves it eligible for the existing payment workflow.
- Route fixed rest-day bonuses to the existing bonus payout stage. Include hourly and GPS hourly earnings in the salary stages. Use a confirmed cutoff for the first installment and a cumulative total for the final installment, crediting any amount already issued. Review unresolved inputs before authorizing the affected stage. Preserve paid records and route later corrections through an explicit audited reconciliation for a future unpaid stage or separately authorized payment. Background calculation never records or sends a payment.

## Confirmed business decisions (2026-11-27)

| Topic                      | Confirmed rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixed bonus amount         | Add the full `130%` daily amount as a bonus (`(agreed monthly salary / scheduled working days in earning month) * 1.30`, Option 1A). No minimum duration is required to qualify (normal 8-hour reference); sessions shorter than 4 hours raise a review warning for HR/Admin.     |
| Fixed schedule and absence | Use scheduled working dates in the full earning month as the divisor. Missing schedule history, zero divisor, partial months, or mid-month term changes route to review. Missing attendance on scheduled days without approved leave flags a review item with no auto deduction.  |
| Rest-day switches          | Two independent per-employee switches toggled by HR, Admin, and Owner (Accountant can view; employee cannot self-toggle). `Auto rest-day bonus` is effective-dated and defaults to **OFF**. `Rest-day clock-in allowed` defaults to **ON**; when OFF, self clock-in on rest days is blocked on the server with a Georgian message while open sessions can still close and HR/Admin can assist or correct. |
| Hourly allowance & bands   | No premium stacking; one multiplier per segment using priority: `GPS job (1.30x + 20m)` -> `Rest day (1.30x)` -> `Night 00:00-10:00 (1.30x)` -> `Evening 21:00-00:00 (1.10x)` -> `Daytime 10:00-21:00 (1.00x)`. Daytime hours beyond `scheduled + 2h` calculate at `1.00x` and flag for HR/Admin review. |
| GPS qualification & sleep  | Active mapped jobs (`pending`, `en_route`, `on_site`) with coordinates, enabled tracker, and assigned crew qualify. Anti-drift dwell: 3-minute entry (or stationary entry into sleep), 5-minute exit. Sleep mode while inside the zone never creates a fake exit; working duration runs from confirmed entry until confirmed exit. Award `+20 minutes` once per job per day per eligible hourly crew member. When a crew member has a conflicting/open manual punch, record the conflict and retain the detected on-site job duration so HR/Admin can resolve it. Start with observation mode before enabling automatic punches. |
| Review before payout       | **Review by Exception (Option A):** Clean automatic calculations are payout-ready (still editable/overridable by HR/Admin with required reason and audit trail). Flagged/anomalous items (missing scheduled days, short rest-day shifts, open/auto-closed sessions, excess daytime hours, GPS conflicts/signal issues) block payout until HR/Admin resolves them. |
