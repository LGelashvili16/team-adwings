# Payroll configuration and delivery

Employee compensation is authorized product scope. The first delivered workflow is `/manage/payroll/settings`, titled `ხელფასების პარამეტრები`, and the `accountant` role labeled `ბუღალტერი`.

Use the [implementation instructions and checked progress plan](payroll-implementation-plan.md) to continue delivery. It records completed work, independently verifiable next steps and remaining decisions.

## Monthly payroll and payment history

1. Open `/manage/payroll/settings` and save a schedule. Choose one or two salary payments per earning month and set their date windows. Bonuses and premiums default to the following month, with the earning month available in the month selector. Choose whether fines are usually taken from the final salary or the bonuses and premiums run. Days beyond the end of a short month use its last day.
2. Set each employee's take-home salary or hourly rate in `/manage/payroll` and confirm their employment dates on their employee profile. The monthly table shows the applicable agreement without requiring a draft. Full fixed agreements supply the base and total automatically. Partial months and changing terms require a confirmed base. Hourly employees need confirmed hours. Open any eligible employee row to edit the monthly base before publication. Enter cumulative hours when there are two payments. Hourly pay rounds to tetri once. Overtime and rest-day formulas are pending.
3. Enter bonuses, premiums and fines in **ბონუსები, პრემიები და ჯარიმები**. Bonus and premium notes are optional. Fine notes are required. Both sheets let you choose a date for the employee's bonus and premium run and for fines deducted from salary. Fines assigned to the bonus run share its date. Select a date in the earning or following month on or after the opening of the associated payment window. Leaving it empty uses the company schedule. The adjustments table keeps one row per employee and earning month and shows the scheduled window or date until a payment posts. It then shows the actual recorded date.
4. Open **ხელფასის გაცემა** from the monthly payroll tab and choose a stage. The preview shows ready employees and the amounts to pay. It explains why other employees are pending. Confirm after paying externally. The app records the confirmation date in the company timezone. Later runs can pick up employees who become ready. Recording opens on the first day of the window and remains available afterward.
5. The first fixed payment defaults to half the monthly base, rounded down to the nearest tetri. The monthly sheet lets you enter a custom first amount. Final salary subtracts the recorded first payment. Hourly pay uses confirmed hours up to the first run and cumulative hours for the final run. A fine that would make its stage negative leaves the employee pending.
6. Employees see published payments in `/payroll`, grouped by earning month with a calendar filter and pagination. Each month shows base salary, bonuses, premiums, fines, total and payment dates. Earlier finalized statements appear in the archive. Older gross agreements stay in history and require a take-home rate before a new payment can be published.

Correcting an applicable fixed salary refreshes an unpaid full-month base that followed the agreement. A partial month or a separately entered amount needs review before payment. The partial-month form prefills the corrected agreement when the previous confirmation matched the old rate. Recorded installments and the draft history remain available at their original amounts.

Payroll management is restricted to administrators, accountants and owners. Route and service permissions are checked independently, including on retries. An actor's own payment is skipped. The personal salary workspace derives the employee from the session and keeps administrative notes and author details private.

For a split schedule, Monthly Payroll can show the entire earning month, the first salary payment or the second salary payment. The installment views show available planned amounts and use the recorded amount after payment. The sheet always edits the full month and has a separate first-payment override. For a partial month or a mid-month rate change, it prefills the applicable fixed salary as an editable starting amount and requires saving the month before payment. Change history starts collapsed. Separately paid bonuses and premiums appear in the whole-month view. CSV export uses the selected period, search and sort across all matching pages, up to 1,000 employees. It includes the earning month, the selected view’s amounts, the amount already issued, and separate scheduled start, scheduled end and recorded payment dates for its payment stages. Dates use `YYYY-MM-DD` and follow the company schedule or the applicable employee-specific date.

If employment dates need attention, the monthly sheet links to the employment periods on the employee profile for users with personnel access. A missing salary agreement links to that employee's salary page.

On a split schedule, the current earning month opens on `1/2` on company-local days 1 through 15 and on `2/2` from day 16. The automatic view refreshes at the half-month boundary. Selecting a tab explicitly keeps that choice in the URL. Earlier and future months default to the full-month view.

Settings saves append audited versions and reject stale edits. Each earning month keeps its schedule version after the first payment. Publishing rechecks current eligibility, salary sources and the reviewed preview in a transaction. Month and request locks serialize competing commands. Each employee can receive each stage once per earning month. Published amounts and dates are immutable. Unpublished amounts remain editable. Legacy closed months are read-only.

Migration `0072_payroll_payment_runs.sql` links payment records to their employee, schedule version and actor. It adds unique employee/month/stage and monthly/retry indexes. It removes retired date, divisor, gross/net default, tax and pension configuration fields and retains historical audit metadata, salary agreements and monthly revisions. Bank transfers, statutory calculations and paid-payment reversals are outside the current workflow.

Migration `0073_payroll_adjustment_dates.sql` adds optional payment dates to the existing monthly revision. Historical rows without a date use the relevant schedule window for pending payouts. Confirmed dates follow payment history.

## Georgian legal baseline

Checked 2026-11-24 against the [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717), [Funded Pension Law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4280127), and [Labour Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1155567). Standard employment-income tax is 20%, subject to applicable exemptions. For pension participants, the standard employee and employer contributions are 2% each. The employer contribution is an additional company expense. Ordinary employment bonuses and premiums require employment-income classification; their names do not establish exemptions. Statutory calculations are handled externally.

Before activating statutory calculations, obtain accountant-reviewed examples for tax bases, pension eligibility/exemptions, sick pay, vacation pay, overtime, gross-up agreements, rounding, deductions and reporting dates. A fine must not automatically reduce taxable earnings. A missing attendance record is not proof of an unlawful absence or a lawful deduction.

## Deferred automation roadmap

These earlier stages are retained for reference and are not prerequisites for the simplified delivery. Add them only when explicitly requested.

1. Reconcile the retained monthly/hourly compensation terms with confirmed employment periods, joining/leaving and rehires for monthly drafts. Keep payroll payable-time classification separate from recorded timesheet totals; resolve open, provisional, pending and rejected attendance explicitly.
2. Build monthly drafts with daily calculation explanations. Detect potential absences only after expected work ends and after checking approved/pending leave, holidays, overnight attendance and employer/system exceptions. Warnings never deduct money without a separate authorized decision.
3. Add employee statutory profiles and exact gross-to-net calculations. Separate employee pension withholding, income tax, lawful other deductions and employer contributions. Record legal policy versions and actual calculation inputs on each statement. Revalidate deduction bases and aggregate limits against the calculated payroll before applying anything.
4. Add independent approval/publication permissions, no self-approval, immutable published statements and auditable subsequent adjustments. Consume approved award/deduction versions once and lock consumed decisions atomically. Retain both earning periods and actual payment/reporting dates. Tax/pension declaration and remittance integrations are separate workflows.
5. Reconcile a shadow payroll month against accountant calculations before operational use. Do not fabricate historical compensation or employment dates.

## Retained individual schedule history

Migration `0055_employee_schedule_history.sql` establishes one observed opening version for each existing employee, using the company-local migration date and their current individual schedule or explicit company inheritance. The source is `migration`, with no invented human editor or earlier effective date. For production rollout, pause schedule writes between the migration and release of the new writer so an older application cannot make unrecorded changes during that interval.

New account provisioning records an inherited or individual opening decision in the account transaction. Operator-only owner bootstrap is explicitly attributed as provisioning without a human actor. Schedule changes and removal of a custom week append versions in the same transaction as the existing current assignment, notifications and access audit. Employee row locks serialize version allocation; a failed history insert rolls the assignment back. Identical current-state saves do not create changes. Every same-day change is retained, and the highest version provides that date's reference. Already-started attendance sessions retain their own original expectation snapshots.

The private `getHistoricalEmployeeSchedule` service validates an employee UUID and inclusive date range of at most 366 days. One SQL snapshot resolves retained employee decisions against the company schedule effective on each date. It returns daily reference hours and distinguishes `unknown_history`, `unconfigured`, `scheduled` and `day_off`. Inheritance is a known decision; an absent earlier version is unknown. Missing company weekday rows and malformed retained individual weeks cannot become known days off. Callers must establish the actor's permissions and target-employee scope before this internal read, as with existing attendance/leave scheduling interfaces. It has no independently exposed HTTP or server-function endpoint.

This supplies schedule references for future calculations, without applying leave, holiday exceptions, absence decisions, payable-time classification or money calculations. Current attendance and vacation-allowance workflows continue to use their existing current-assignment behavior; this release does not recalculate historical leave charges.

## Confirmed employment periods

Employee profiles at `/manage/employees/$employeeId` now compose the `employees` feature's employment-period card. Readers need `manage.access` and `employees.manage`; mutations also need `employees.employment.manage`, granted to HR, administrators and owners by default. Accountants retain their separate payroll-configuration scope. Editing one's own periods is forbidden, including for owners. HR can confirm business dates for an administrator/accountant without receiving authority over that person's roles, account access or recovery codes.

Each confirmed period has inclusive company-local start/end dates; an empty end means ongoing employment. Separate non-overlapping periods support partial months and rehires. Planned dates are allowed and labeled. Authorized managers can enter an optional start date when creating an employee account. That date creates an audited, open-ended employment period in the same transaction. Leaving it blank keeps eligibility unknown until a manager confirms the dates on the employee profile. Activation does not set employment dates. Neither suspension nor the existing employee access status supplies an end date. Saving or voiding a period changes no access state and does not schedule a future account suspension.

The right-side sheet uses a native slide-in and shows date guidance in icon alerts. It retains failed drafts and captured revisions, prevents duplicate saves or dismissal while pending, and stays mounted through the close transition. The period table uses TanStack Table with shared shadcn presentation. `employmentPage` and `employmentHistoryPage` are independently validated, one-based URL state. Each server page contains at most five entries. The server records an audit note for creation, correction and voiding. History retains before and after dates, operation, actor, timestamp, and earlier explanations. Voiding excludes a period from eligibility and retains its audit.

Migration `0056_employee_employment_periods.sql` adds period/audit tables and the employee-wide revision, with no fabricated legacy periods. The service re-reads and share-locks current actor authorization, locks the target employee, rejects stale revisions and inclusive overlaps, and commits the dates, revision and audit atomically. Database checks also enforce date bounds and one non-voided open period per employee.

The private `readEmploymentEligibility(tx, input)` service reads at most 366 calendar dates and returns a revision and confirmed overlapping intervals, or an explicit `unknown` state when no authoritative periods exist. The caller must authorize the employee and use its own consistent transaction. Confirmed dates are supplied by HR; legacy completeness is never inferred from the account. Payment preparation rejects unknown eligibility and revalidates employment before publication; automatic daily earnings reconciliation remains deferred. Employment periods alone do not determine payable days, prove absence or authorize a deduction.

## Salary workspace and assignment

Every signed-in employee has `ხელფასი` at `/payroll`. It shows the current agreed monthly amount or hourly rate, next scheduled change, paginated rate history and published payroll by earning month. The calendar-month filter and pagination run on the server.

Administrators, accountants and owners can open `ხელფასები` at `/manage/payroll` with `manage.access` and `payroll.read.all`. The directory has validated URL search, employment-status/type filters, name ordering, SQL pagination and separate counts for monthly/hourly/unconfigured employees. The full-width detail at `/manage/payroll/$userId` shows an individual employee’s current agreement, scheduled change and administrative history. Rate setting additionally requires `payroll.terms.manage`. Self-assignment is forbidden, including for owners. The company configuration remains at `/manage/payroll/settings`, and the salary directory, monthly payroll, adjustments and settings pages share section navigation.

The directory filters sit directly above the table. Open an employee’s salary detail from the table to assign their salary terms. The Sheet captures each target's expected version and requires salary type, exact take-home amount in lari and an effective date. The salary editor records an automatic audit note, and existing reasons remain in history. Failed saves retain the draft. Pending saves block repeated submission and dismissal. No salary is guessed or seeded from attendance or account records.

Migration `0057_employee_salary_terms.sql` adds immutable salary-term versions. Dates are inclusive company-local dates. For a date, the greatest effective date on or before it wins, with the greatest version breaking same-day ties. Later edits for earlier dates retain existing future changes. To correct a scheduled agreement, record the replacement at its effective date. Exact integer tetri and explicit `GEL` currency are persisted; decimal text is validated before conversion. Lari formatting stays Georgian even when a browser lacks Georgian ICU currency support.

Writers recheck current persisted authorization, lock target employees in a deterministic order, compare expected versions and insert the full audited agreement in one transaction. Bulk assignments either all commit or all roll back. No mutation changes a prior agreement. The simple monthly workspace snapshots dated salary terms, checks confirmed employment and revalidates sources at publication. Partial months and changing rates use a confirmed base. Backdated changes preserve historical statements and published payments.

## Retired deduction review

The former deduction review routes redirect to the combined adjustments tab. The old proposal and decision tables are retained for audit, including original relationships and history. Their amounts are never imported into payroll automatically because some may already have been entered manually. The unified workspace uses the existing monthly payroll permissions and tables.
