# Payroll configuration and delivery

Employee compensation is authorized product scope. The first delivered workflow is `/manage/payroll/settings`, titled `ხელფასების პარამეტრები`, and the `accountant` role labeled `ბუღალტერი`.

Use the [implementation instructions and checked progress plan](payroll-implementation-plan.md) to continue delivery. It records completed work, independently verifiable next steps and remaining decisions.

## Simple monthly payroll

Delivery was narrowed on 2026-11-25 to fixed/hourly salaries, bonuses, premiums, fines and monthly finalization. Open `/manage/payroll/monthly`, select a month and open an employee's row. Payroll editors can search by employee name or username and open rows with or without a saved entry in an open month. Enter confirmed hours for hourly pay and simple amount/reason lines, then save. Fixed salaries default to the applicable full monthly agreement. Hourly pay multiplies the exact rate by accountant-confirmed seconds and rounds once, half up, to tetri. Hours accept two decimal places; raw attendance is not automatically payable time.

Preparing base pay requires confirmed employment and an applicable dated salary. Partial months, rehires/gaps and changing rates require an explicitly confirmed base amount. The server records an audit note automatically. This is an accountant input, with no automatic proration, absence penalties, leave valuation or overtime engine. A month that changes between gross and net bases is blocked rather than summed. Prepared rows retain their agreement basis. The displayed contractual total is base plus bonuses and premiums minus fines; base earnings stay separate from fines. No withholding, pension, gross-up, legal deduction-limit validation, payment or tax declaration is performed. Company calculation switches do not activate an engine in this workspace. Accountants remain responsible for checking actual contractual amounts and lawful deductions before operational payment.

Reading requires `manage.access` and `payroll.read.all`. Writing, removal, finalization and reopening additionally require `payroll.manage`, defaulting only to administrators, accountants and owners. Preparers can finalize other employees' entries without a separate proposal review stage. Editing one's own entry and finalizing/reopening a month containing one's own active entry are forbidden. Current persisted grants and account/employee access state are checked inside each transaction, including retries.

A saved employee/month has one current revision and retained immutable history. Bonus and premium lines take an optional note. Fine lines require a reason. Adjustment amounts must be positive. Pending drafts can show a negative adjustment subtotal when fines exceed additions. Prepared payroll totals must stay between zero and the existing monetary limit. Saves compare captured revisions and salary/employment fingerprints. Month-level locks serialize saves, removal and decisions. Identical actor/command retries replay once; changed retries fail. Finalization revalidates every active row and locks the entire month's saved entries atomically, including against new additions. Reopening requires a reason and preserves the finalized revisions. Erroneous draft entries can be removed with a reason and restored through a new save without deleting their history. Months contain at most 1,000 employee identities and each entry at most 20 adjustment lines.

The payroll tabs contain monthly payroll, employee rates, **ბონუსები, პრემიები და ჯარიმები**, and settings. The adjustments tab lists each employee’s bonus, premium, fine and net adjustment totals for the selected month. Both payroll views use the same monthly entry and calendar picker, so each adjustment is counted once.

Use **დამატება** to select an employee and enter a type and amount for the selected month. Every line uses **შენიშვნა**, which is required for fines. All three can be recorded before base pay is prepared in Monthly Payroll. Each employee has one row per month, with separate subtotals for the three types. Use **რედაქტირება** in the row menu to update individual entries or remove them with the trash button. **ჩანაწერების ამოღება** clears all three types for that employee and month after confirmation, preserving base pay, confirmed hours and audit history.

Migration `0067_unified_monthly_adjustments.sql` extends adjustment-only drafts to fines, preserves the finalized-payroll constraints and aligns the month/employee/revision index with latest-revision queries.

Migration `0068_payroll_employee_history_index.sql` adds an employee/month/revision index for personal statement history. The existing month-first index remains in use by the management directory and month decisions. The employee query selects each month's latest revision before checking its finalized status.

If the first salary starts partway through the selected month and applies during confirmed employment, confirm the month's base amount in Monthly Payroll. The salary keeps its original effective date.

Migration `0064_simple_monthly_payroll.sql` adds one append-only table without creating employee financial records. The local migration hash was verified. Verification evidence is kept in the current scope section of the [implementation checklist](payroll-implementation-plan.md).

## Delivered configuration

Administrators, accountants and owners receive `manage.access`, `payroll.configure`, `payroll.read.all`, `payroll.terms.manage` and `payroll.manage`. These payroll grants are restricted to those roles. Individual grants cannot confer management payroll on other roles, and explicit denials still apply to eligible roles. Route guards and server functions enforce their respective permissions. Payroll services check persisted roles, overrides, account access state and employee status inside their transactions.

Accountant is a privileged provisionable role alongside administrator. Only actors with `admin.access`, `users.manage` and `roles.manage` may provision or assign it. Ordinary HR employee-management rights cannot grant accountant or reset, edit or suspend an accountant account. Existing administrative role editing retains its self-modification prohibition, session revocation and audits.

The form stores an effective date, default gross/net agreement basis, fixed-monthly daily divisor, income-tax calculation toggle and rate, pension calculation toggle, and separate employee/employer pension rates. The divisor options are scheduled working days, calendar days, or a fixed 30 days; these are contractual settings, not a claim that every selection fits every employment agreement. Suggested percentages are 20%, 2% and 2%. Neither calculation toggle is enabled by the unsaved draft.

Every save creates an immutable `payroll_policy` version with the trusted author, an automatic audit note and a database timestamp. Existing reasons remain in history. This row is also its audit record, committed atomically. An advisory transaction lock serializes first/subsequent saves; the expected version rejects stale drafts. Rates use integer hundredths of a percent, avoiding floating-point persistence. Past-effective writes are rejected using the company-local date. Future policies are retained separately from the currently applicable policy. For a date, select the greatest effective date on or before it, then the greatest version for that date. Several same-day saves preserve every version. A later edit for an earlier effective date does not cancel an existing future-dated policy.

Reads create no policies. Suggested defaults never imply a saved configuration or an employee salary. This configuration screen does not calculate employee pay. The simple monthly workspace above records contractual amounts; withholding and payment remain external. Disabling built-in withholding means external accounting, not exemption from a statutory obligation. Pension participation and tax exemptions will be employee-specific inputs to the calculation workflow.

## Georgian legal baseline

Checked 2026-11-24 against the [Tax Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1043717), [Funded Pension Law](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/4280127), and [Labour Code](https://matsne.gov.ge/ka/document/view/1155567). Standard employment-income tax is 20%, subject to applicable exemptions. For pension participants, the standard employee and employer contributions are 2% each. The employer contribution is an additional company expense. Ordinary employment bonuses and premiums require employment-income classification; their names do not establish exemptions. Policy percentages remain configurable to accommodate lawful applicable rules and changes, but saved settings alone do not establish legal compliance.

Before activating statutory calculations, obtain accountant-reviewed examples for tax bases, pension eligibility/exemptions, sick pay, vacation pay, overtime, gross-up agreements, rounding, deductions and reporting dates. A fine must not automatically reduce taxable earnings. A missing attendance record is not proof of an unlawful absence or a lawful deduction.

## Deferred automation roadmap

These earlier stages are retained for reference and are not prerequisites for the simplified delivery. Add them only when explicitly requested.

1. Reconcile the retained monthly/hourly compensation terms with confirmed employment periods, joining/leaving and rehires for monthly drafts. Keep payroll payable-time classification separate from recorded timesheet totals; resolve open, provisional, pending and rejected attendance explicitly.
2. Build monthly drafts with daily calculation explanations. Detect potential absences only after expected work ends and after checking approved/pending leave, holidays, overnight attendance and employer/system exceptions. Warnings never deduct money without a separate authorized decision.
3. Add employee statutory profiles and exact gross-to-net calculations. Separate employee pension withholding, income tax, lawful other deductions and employer contributions. Record legal policy versions and actual calculation inputs on each statement. Revalidate deduction bases and aggregate limits against the calculated payroll before applying anything.
4. Add independent approval/publication permissions, no self-approval, immutable published statements and auditable subsequent adjustments. Consume approved award/deduction versions once and lock consumed decisions atomically. Retain both earning periods and actual payment/reporting dates. Tax/pension declaration and remittance integrations are separate workflows.
5. Reconcile a shadow payroll month against accountant calculations before operational use. Do not fabricate historical compensation or employment dates.

## Retained individual schedule history

Migration `0055_employee_schedule_history.sql` establishes one observed opening version for each existing employee, using the company-local migration date and their current individual schedule or explicit company inheritance. The source is `migration`, with no invented human editor or earlier effective date. For production rollout, pause schedule writes between the migration and release of the new writer so an older application cannot make unrecorded changes during that interval.

New account provisioning records an inherited or individual opening decision in the account transaction. Operator-only owner bootstrap is explicitly attributed as provisioning without a human actor. Schedule changes and removal of a custom week append versions in the same transaction as the existing current assignment, notifications and access audit. Employee row locks serialize version allocation; a failed history insert rolls the assignment back. Identical current-state saves do not create changes. Every same-day change is retained, and the highest version provides that date's reference. Already-started attendance sessions retain their own original expectation snapshots.

The private `getHistoricalEmployeeSchedule` service validates an employee UUID and inclusive date range of at most 366 days. One SQL snapshot resolves retained employee decisions against the company schedule effective on each date. It returns daily reference hours and distinguishes `unknown_history`, `unconfigured`, `scheduled` and `day_off`. Inheritance is a known decision; an absent earlier version is unknown. Missing company weekday rows and malformed retained individual weeks cannot become known days off. Callers must establish the actor's permissions and target-employee scope before this internal read, as with existing attendance/leave scheduling interfaces. It has no independently exposed HTTP or server-function endpoint.

This supplies schedule references for future calculations, without applying leave, holiday exceptions, absence decisions, payable-time classification or money calculations. Current attendance and vacation-allowance workflows continue to use their existing current-assignment behavior; this release does not recalculate historical leave charges.

## Confirmed employment periods

Employee profiles at `/manage/employees/$employeeId` now compose the `employees` feature's employment-period card. Readers need `manage.access` and `employees.manage`; mutations also need `employees.employment.manage`, granted to HR, administrators and owners by default. Accountants retain their separate payroll-configuration scope. Editing one's own periods is forbidden, including for owners. HR can confirm business dates for an administrator/accountant without receiving authority over that person's roles, account access or recovery codes.

Each confirmed period has inclusive company-local start/end dates; an empty end means ongoing employment. Separate non-overlapping periods support partial months and rehires. Planned dates are allowed and labeled. Authorized managers can enter an optional start date when creating an employee account. That date creates an audited, open-ended employment period in the same transaction. Leaving it blank keeps eligibility unknown until a manager confirms the dates on the employee profile. Activation does not set employment dates. Neither suspension nor the existing employee access status supplies an end date. Saving or voiding a period changes no access state and does not schedule a future account suspension.

The right-side sheet uses a native slide-in and shows date guidance in icon alerts. It retains failed drafts and captured revisions, prevents duplicate saves or dismissal while pending, and stays mounted through the close transition. The period table uses TanStack Table with shared shadcn presentation. `employmentPage` and `employmentHistoryPage` are independently validated, one-based URL state. Each server page contains at most five entries. The server records an audit note for creation, correction and voiding. History retains before and after dates, operation, actor, timestamp, and earlier explanations. Voiding excludes a period from eligibility and retains its audit.

Migration `0056_employee_employment_periods.sql` adds period/audit tables and the employee-wide revision, with no fabricated legacy periods. The service re-reads and share-locks current actor authorization, locks the target employee, rejects stale revisions and inclusive overlaps, and commits the dates, revision and audit atomically. Database checks also enforce date bounds and one non-voided open period per employee.

The private `readEmploymentEligibility(tx, input)` service reads at most 366 calendar dates and returns a revision and confirmed overlapping intervals, or an explicit `unknown` state when no authoritative periods exist. The caller must authorize the employee and use its own consistent transaction. Confirmed dates are supplied by HR; legacy completeness is never inferred from the account. Future statement preparation/finalization must reject unknown eligibility, reconcile complete confirmed periods, snapshot the revision and revalidate it before financial decisions. The simple monthly workspace rechecks employment before finalization; automatic daily earnings reconciliation remains deferred. Employment periods alone do not determine payable days, prove absence or authorize a deduction.

## Salary workspace and assignment

Every signed-in employee has `ხელფასი` at `/payroll`. It shows the current agreed monthly amount or hourly rate, next scheduled change, paginated rate history and finalized monthly statements. Statements show base salary, bonus, premium and fine subtotals, and the total with its contractual gross/net basis. Statement pagination and the calendar-month filter run on the server. The subject comes exclusively from the authenticated identity. Administrative reasons and author details are excluded from self-service responses. A reopened month is hidden until it is finalized again.

Administrators, accountants and owners can open `ხელფასები` at `/manage/payroll` with `manage.access` and `payroll.read.all`. The directory has validated URL search, employment-status/type filters, name ordering, SQL pagination and separate counts for monthly/hourly/unconfigured employees. The full-width detail at `/manage/payroll/$userId` shows an individual employee’s current agreement, scheduled change and administrative history. Rate setting additionally requires `payroll.terms.manage`. Self-assignment is forbidden, including for owners. The company configuration remains at `/manage/payroll/settings`, and the salary directory, monthly payroll, adjustments and settings pages share section navigation.

The directory filters sit directly above the table. Open an employee’s salary detail from the table to assign their salary terms. The Sheet captures each target's expected version and requires salary type, exact amount in lari, gross/net basis and an effective date. The salary editor records an automatic audit note, and existing reasons remain in history. New agreements start with the active company policy's basis; changing that default does not rewrite existing contracts. Failed saves retain the draft. Pending saves block repeated submission and dismissal. No salary is guessed or seeded from attendance or account records.

Migration `0057_employee_salary_terms.sql` adds immutable salary-term versions. Dates are inclusive company-local dates. For a date, the greatest effective date on or before it wins, with the greatest version breaking same-day ties. Later edits for earlier dates retain existing future changes. To correct a scheduled agreement, record the replacement at its effective date. Exact integer tetri and explicit `GEL` currency are persisted; decimal text is validated before conversion. Lari formatting stays Georgian even when a browser lacks Georgian ICU currency support.

Writers recheck current persisted authorization, lock target employees in a deterministic order, compare expected versions and insert the full audited agreement in one transaction. Bulk assignments either all commit or all roll back. No mutation changes a prior agreement. The simple monthly workspace snapshots dated salary terms, checks confirmed employment and revalidates sources at finalization. Partial months and changing rates use an explicitly explained base rather than an automatic calendar engine. Backdated salary entry never rewrites finalized monthly revisions. Reopen a month and resave affected entries to apply a correction.

Management records monthly contractual amounts in the monthly workspace. Employees see their own finalized amounts and salary agreements. Finalization makes the statement visible and does not record payment or compute withholding.

## Retired deduction review

The former deduction review routes redirect to the combined adjustments tab. The old proposal and decision tables are retained for audit, including original relationships and history. Their amounts are never imported into payroll automatically because some may already have been entered manually. The unified workspace uses the existing monthly payroll permissions and tables.
